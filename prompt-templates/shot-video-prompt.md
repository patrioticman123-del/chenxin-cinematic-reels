# Google Flow — 8s Video Prompt 模板

每次依主題產出 **4 組獨立完整英文 prompt**；Flow 生成時長預設 8 秒、9:16 portrait。最後一段使用 6 秒，成片 30 秒。需按選定模型確認 8 秒與 Ingredients/Frames 是否支援。

```text
Cinematic photorealistic 3D medical explainer, vertical portrait 9:16, continuous eight-second shot.
Doctor: Same physician as the provided authorized reference photo, young East Asian man with short neatly styled black hair, warm expression, slightly stylized realistic face, white medical coat, pale-blue shirt, navy diagonal-striped tie, dark trousers. Identity and clothing remain unchanged across all clips.
Scene: 【anatomically plausible setting relevant to user topic】
Visitor: 【consistent audience surrogate, if needed】
Action: 【walking / turning / pointing / reaction with start and end poses for 8 seconds】
Camera: 【tracking shot / dolly-in / macro push / stable realistic motion】
Continuity: 【use same doctor photo; reference previous clip's saved frame if supported】
Lighting: warm soft rim light, cinematic depth of field, tactile realistic textures.
Anatomy: 【medically reviewed structure and correct relationship】
Audio: ambient sounds only; Taiwanese Mandarin male narration will be added in post.
No text, subtitles, watermarks, logos, uncanny face changes, deformed hands, extra fingers, inaccurate anatomy, aliens, or still-photo zoom tricks.
```

另外寫出：本段 Flow 生成秒數 8、成片剪輯秒數（8/8/8/6）、中文台詞與字幕、參考素材、接續鏡頭和功能不支援時的備用方式。
